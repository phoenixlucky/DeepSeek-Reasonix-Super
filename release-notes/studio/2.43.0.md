## New

- Settings gain Messages between sessions (experimental): list other local sessions, send them text, and approve what arrives in a bar above the composer. Off by default. Refs #11376.
- With messages between sessions on, the model gains list_peers and send_peer_message; sending asks you first and shows the recipient and full text, and the other user must approve. Refs #11376.
- The sidebar search now also looks through the text of conversations in every project once you pause, listing the matching conversations with a passage; click one to open it. Refs #11942.
- On a wide display (2400 px or more) at 100% scaling, Studio asks once whether the interface is too small and offers 125% or 150% with a restore; it never resizes by itself. Refs #11962.
- The dot on each model source remembers its last test: red for a failed one with the cause on hover, a hollow ring for a pass, cleared when the address or key changes. Refs #12040.
- A tool call still running after 10 seconds offers "Stop only this call": it ends that call, the turn goes on, and the model is told you stopped it. Refs #12254.
- Pictures in a conversation also load from a folder you granted this workspace (Settings, Sandbox, extra folders); local pictures elsewhere still show a placeholder. Refs #13125.
